Use a fenced `screen <name> <title>` block for a rough wireframe of a screen a person sees. Give each screen a unique one-word name using letters, digits, hyphens, or underscores.

Write one part per line: `title: text`, `text: text`, `chip: text`, `button: text`, `field: text`, or `list: item | item`. A `row:` has no text of its own; put its parts below it, indented by two spaces. Only `row:` can contain nested parts. Mark a new or changed part with `(new)` or `(changed)` at the end.

Add a numbered dot such as `(1)` to a part when it needs explanation, then put one `1: Bold lead. Rest.` note under the closing fence for that dot. Keep to 2 screens, 14 parts and 5 dots per screen.
